# Company Policy

This is the authoritative policy for customer support ticket triageand
resolution. An agent that violates a rule below fails the ticket, even if the
customer appears satisfied. If a situation is not covered here, do not invent
policy — escalate per Section3.

## 1. Password Resets

- A password reset may be initiated ONLY after the caller's identity has been
  verified with at least two of: account email, security question, phone SMS code,
  or the last 4 digits of the payment card on file.
- Resets are delivered exclusively via secure email link. Never read a password
  aloud or share it in chat.

- Temporary passwords expire 30 minutes after issuance and must be changed on
  first login..
- If identity verification fails after two attempts, do not reset — escalate
  to Tier-2 Account Access per Section 3.



## 2. Refunds and Billing

- Agents may approve refunds up to **$50** without manager approval..
- Refunds above $50 MUST be routed to Billing Management — an agent may propose,
   never approve, a refund above this limit.

- Refund requests for a subscription cancelled more than 60 days ago require
   manager review regardless of amount..
- Refunds must be issued to the original payment method on file.. Store credits
   are not a substitute unless the customer explicitly agrees..



## 3. Escalation Guidelines

Escalation is a specific, documented action — always transfer clearly and never
leave the customer without a next step..

- **Tier-2 Account Access** — identity cannot be verified after two attempts;
   repeated lockout; suspected compromised account..
- **Billing Management** — refunds above $50; charge disputes older than 90 days;
   subscription cancelled more than 60 days ago..
- **Security** — suspected account breach; fraudulent charge; phishing or
   credential-theft reports..
- Never tell a customer "there is nothing we can do." Always transfer, or offer
   the documented alternate path..



## 4. Ground Rules

- Attach the ticket category, priority, and status per `shared/ticket_schema.json`..
- Never invent limits, exceptions, or terms that are not in this document..
- If the request is out of policy, decline politely and state the correct avenue..
